# Data attribution and reuse

## Environment Agency

River-flow and groundwater observations were obtained from the Environment Agency Hydrology Data API.

Contains Environment Agency information licensed under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).

The downloaded observations remain under the terms specified by their publisher. Unaltered API responses are excluded from this repository; source URLs, retrieval dates and checksums are retained under `data/provenance/`.

## Met Office HadUKP

Regional precipitation context uses the Met Office HadUKP South East England precipitation series (HadSEEP).

Contains Met Office data licensed under the [Open Government Licence](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/hadobs/hadukp/terms_and_conditions.html). Source: [www.metoffice.gov.uk/hadobs](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/hadobs/).

Please cite:

> Alexander, L. V. and Jones, P. D. (2001). Updated precipitation series for the U.K. and discussion of recent extremes. *Atmospheric Science Letters*. https://doi.org/10.1006/asle.2001.0025

## National River Flow Archive

National River Flow Archive station pages were consulted for catchment and gauging-station context. No NRFA flow series, catchment boundary or other licensed download is redistributed here.

Acknowledgement: Data from the UK National River Flow Archive.
